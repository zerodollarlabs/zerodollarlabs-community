# Our community code

Be kind, clear and respectful. Critique ideas and software, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, spam and sharing private information without consent are not welcome.

Keep bug reports reproducible and suggestions constructive. Redact private diagnostics before posting. Moderators may edit or remove content, lock discussions or restrict participation to protect the community. Report concerns privately using the contact address on our website. We review reports with care and respect confidentiality where possible.
