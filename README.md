# Zero Dollar Labs Community

A public place for ideas, questions and reproducible bug reports about our small native Mac tools. Notch is free to download and use. Payments and paid memberships are not enabled.

## Where to post

- [Report a bug](https://github.com/zerodollarlabs/zerodollarlabs-community/issues/new?template=bug.yml&tool=Notch).
- [Suggest a tool or feature](https://github.com/zerodollarlabs/zerodollarlabs-community/issues/new?template=idea.yml).
- [Ask a question](https://github.com/zerodollarlabs/zerodollarlabs-community/issues/new?template=question.yml).
- Once Discussions are enabled, [join a discussion](https://github.com/zerodollarlabs/zerodollarlabs-community/discussions).

The website's Community page is the public entry point. App source, signing keys and release storage are kept in separate private repositories. This repository does not distribute app binaries.

## Keep reports safe

This is a **public** repository. Never post passwords, access tokens, private keys, personal clipboard contents, full unredacted logs, bank information or other people's personal information. Review Diagnostics before pasting it; remove personal paths, city/location data and identifying information. We do not need screenshots of your Keychain or payment accounts.

For security vulnerabilities, use the private reporting instructions in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public issue. For private support, email zerodollarlabs@gmail.com.

## Participation

Be kind, specific and patient. Follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). GitHub sign-in is required to open issues or discussions. Email does not require a GitHub account.
